Privacy Policy

← Back to the app

Last updated 2026-08-25.

Yoto Radio Station Picker ("this app") is a small, static, open-source tool that turns an internet radio station into a Yoto MYO card. It has no backend server and no database of its own — it runs entirely in your browser.

What this app does not do

  • It does not have user accounts, cookies, analytics, or trackers.
  • It does not collect, log, or store your personal data anywhere. There is no server to store it on.
  • It never sees or handles your Yoto password — you log in on Yoto's own login page.

What data is involved, and where it goes

  • Station search. Your search terms (station name, country, tag) are sent directly from your browser to radio-browser.info's public API. This app never sees or relays that traffic — it's a direct browser-to-radio-browser.info request.
  • Yoto login. Logging in redirects you to Yoto's own login page (an OAuth2 flow with PKCE). Yoto issues an access token (and optionally a refresh token) back to your browser, which this app stores in your browser's localStorage so you don't have to log in every visit. Those tokens are only ever sent to Yoto's own API, straight from your browser — never to any server this app controls, because none exists.
  • Card creation. When you build a card, the station details you selected are sent directly from your browser to Yoto's content API to create the card on your account.
  • Fonts. The page font (Fredoka) loads from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention

Your Yoto tokens live only in your own browser's local storage until you click "Log out" or clear your browser's site data. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because this app has nowhere else to retain it.

Children's privacy

Yoto is a product designed for children, but this app itself does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, child or adult. All it does is help you point a Yoto card at a radio stream.

Questions

This is an independent, unofficial project — not affiliated with Yoto Ltd. Source code, and a place to raise questions or issues, is on GitHub.
